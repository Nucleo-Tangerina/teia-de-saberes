# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js (App Router, TypeScript), chosen by the user. Plain global CSS, `next/font` for Manrope + Inter, `lucide-react` icons.

## Users

Primary: Secretarias Municipais de Educação, secretários de Educação, gestores públicos and equipes técnicas, evaluating a partner to support planning, training and management of their municipal school networks. Secondary: gestores escolares and educators looking for formações and pós-graduação.

## Product Purpose

Institutional site for Teia de Saberes: present the organization, its educational solutions, team and experiences, and make it easy for a municipal manager to start a conversation ("Fale com nossa equipe").

## Positioning

Concept: "Conectando saberes. Fortalecendo a gestão. Transformando a educação." Main message: "Uma educação pública mais forte começa com uma gestão preparada." Teia works alongside municipal teams, building paths together and respecting each network's reality, bringing academic knowledge close to the concrete needs of the redes de ensino.

## Operating Context

Visitors are public officials, often on desktop at the secretaria or on mobile between meetings, comparing assessorias. Contact happens via form (to be built with a real backend), WhatsApp (13) 99208-1120 and Instagram @teia.de.saberes.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Home first (10 blocks + fixed header + footer); internal pages (/quem-somos, /solucoes-educacionais, /mentoria-gestao-publica, /mentoria-transformacao-escolar, /palestras-formacoes, /pos-graduacao, /nossa-equipe, /projetos, /blog, /contato) come later.
- Solutions listed in the approved design: Tear (Plano Municipal de Educação, tear.teiadesaberes.com.br), Caminhos da Inclusão (caminhoinclusao.teiadesaberes.com.br), the four mentorias/formações/pós from the brief, and three complementary assessorias (técnica SME, prestação de contas, caixa escolar). Current offer to be confirmed before publishing.
- Contact form must send for real (no simulated submit), with validation, antispam and LGPD consent. Not built yet.

## Brand Commitments

- Official logo used as-is (`public/logo.png`, `public/logo-white.png`); never redrawn.
- Geometric pattern strip (`public/pattern-strip.png`) is the brand's signature motif.
- Suggested palette (to validate): azul institucional #153A62, azul secundário #2679AD, laranja #ED8C32, fundo #F4F7FA, branco. Titles Manrope, body Inter.
- Voice: institutional, clear, welcoming, technical without jargon, no unproven promises.
- The editorial brief (copy per block) is the source of truth; copy is not rewritten.

## Evidence on Hand

- Partner municipalities with crests (19, MG/RJ) in `public/brasoes/`, provided in the approved design.
- Photos in `public/images/` are placeholders from the design round, not authorized institutional photos; replace before launch.
- Absent, must not be fabricated: testimonials, numbers/results, team names and CVs, project cases, article authors/dates, official e-mail.

## Product Principles

1. Clarity for public managers beats spectacle: they must know what Teia does and how to talk to it within seconds.
2. Only verified facts are published; pending content is visibly marked "a validar".
3. Connection is the brand: people, knowledge and municipalities woven together.
4. Every path ends in a conversation with the team.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Adequate contrast, keyboard navigation, alt text, form labels, reduced-motion respect. Audience includes public servants on varied devices and connections; mobile performance matters.
